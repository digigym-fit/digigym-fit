Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DigiGym ("DigiGym", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the DigiGym mobile application (package name com.digigym.fit, the "App"). The App is used both by gym members and by the gyms that run them, and this policy covers both.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use the App:

  • Phone number — used to sign you in via a one-time password (OTP), or via Truecaller verification on Android if you choose it. We do not use email/password accounts. Your mobile number is also how the App recognises you as a member of a gym.
  • Profile information — full name, age, gender, height, and date of birth, which you provide when setting up your DigiGym profile.
  • Membership and payment records — the gyms you belong to, the plans recorded against your name, the amount paid, the payment mode, and the dates a plan runs from and until. These are entered by your gym; DigiGym does not process payments.
  • Check-in records — the date and time of each visit to a gym, and the method used to check in (QR code or mobile number).
  • Information your gym provides about you — if a gym registers you at their desk, they may record your name, age, gender, address, and an emergency contact (name, phone number, and relationship to you). If you later install the App with the same mobile number, these records are matched to your account.

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, your photo library, your contacts, or health data from Health Connect, Apple Health, or similar services. The App does not include any advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Camera

The App uses the camera on one screen only: the gym's check-in stand, where staff scan a member's on-screen QR code. The camera reads the check-in code and nothing else. No photo or video is captured, stored, or uploaded. Members do not need to grant camera access.

Truecaller Sign-In (Android)

On Android, you may choose to sign in with Truecaller instead of an SMS code. If you do, Truecaller shows you its own consent screen and, with your agreement, returns a single-use code that we exchange for your verified phone number. This is optional — the SMS one-time password is always available, and is the only method on iOS. Truecaller's handling of your information is governed by its own privacy policy.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure (phone number + OTP, or Truecaller verification).
  • To create and display your fitness profile within the App.
  • To show you your gym membership, when it expires, and the payments recorded against it.
  • To let you check in at your gym, and to show you your own visit history.
  • To let the gym you belong to manage its membership records and admit you at the door.

How Information Is Stored

After you sign in, an authentication token is stored securely on your device and is used to keep you signed in. Your profile, membership, and check-in information is stored on DigiGym's servers and associated with your mobile number.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Information is shared in the following limited ways:

  • With your gym. The gym you are a member of can see the records it holds about you — your name, age, gender, contact and emergency details, your plan and payment history at that gym, and your check-ins at that gym. A gym cannot see your activity at any other gym.
  • With our SMS provider. Your phone number is passed to our SMS provider (Message Central) solely to deliver your one-time password.
  • With Truecaller, only if you choose Truecaller sign-in, as described above.

Information may also be disclosed if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of DigiGym, our users, or others.

Data Retention

We retain your account and profile information for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see Delete Account & Data.

Your Rights

You can request to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at digigym.fit@gmail.com.

Age Requirement and Children's Privacy

DigiGym is not directed at children. You must be at least 18 years old to create a DigiGym account and use the App, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it.

If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, contact us at digigym.fit@gmail.com and we will remove the account and its data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at digigym.fit@gmail.com.
