Common questions

The things members and gym owners ask before they start.

What is DigiGym?

DigiGym is gym membership and check-in software. Members use it to see their plan and payment history, to check in at their gym with a QR code, and to review their own visit history. Gyms use it to keep their member roster, record plans and payments, set their opening hours, and admit members at the door.

How do I check in at my gym?

Open the app and show the check-in code on your screen. It is valid for sixty seconds and only at that one gym, and it is used up once the front desk scans it. If you would rather not use the app, the desk can check you in by your mobile number instead.

Do I need to install the app to be a member?

No. Your gym can register you at the desk in under a minute, and you can then check in by mobile number without ever installing DigiGym. If you do install it later with the same number, your existing membership and payment records are matched to your account.

Does DigiGym take my membership payment?

No. DigiGym does not process payments. You pay your gym the way you always have, and the gym records that payment against your plan so you can see it in the app. Your membership contract is with your gym, not with DigiGym.

How do I sign in?

With your mobile number and a one-time password sent by SMS — there is no password to remember. On Android you can instead verify in one tap with Truecaller if you prefer.

Is there a minimum age to use DigiGym?

Yes. You must be at least 18 years old to create a DigiGym account and use the app.
