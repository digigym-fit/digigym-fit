Delete Account & Data

Last updated: August 13, 2026

You can request deletion of your DigiGym account and all associated data at any time.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to digigym.fit@gmail.com from the phone number registered on your account, with the subject line "Delete My Account". Include the phone number associated with your DigiGym account so we can locate it.

We will verify your request and delete your account within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile information (full name, age, gender, height, date of birth)
  • Your phone number and authentication data
  • Your check-in history
  • Any other data associated with your account on DigiGym's servers

Records Held By Your Gym

Separately from your DigiGym account, the gym you are a member of holds its own membership record for you — your contact and emergency details, your plan, and the payments recorded against it. Deleting your DigiGym account removes your access to the App; your gym's own membership and payment records are kept by the gym as its business records, and may be retained where it needs them for accounting or legal reasons. To have those removed, contact your gym directly, or tell us in your deletion request and we will pass the request on.

Data We May Retain

We may retain limited information where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Any retained data is kept only as long as necessary for these purposes.

Questions

If you have questions about this process, contact us at digigym.fit@gmail.com.
